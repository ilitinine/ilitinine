## Ibrahim Litinine

I am Ibrahim Litinine and I like to think of myself as a website growth manager.
Content marketing is the job I typically get hired to do and it is still the
centre of everything. The title stopped covering the work once the job stopped
meaning only writing and posting articles.

In practice that means most of my week goes into blog posts. Most SEO content
chases search volume and reaches people who are only reading. I write for the
ones already trying to fix the problem, a smaller audience that converts far
better. The post has to earn the next step on the strength of its argument
rather than a call to action bolted onto the end.

Writing is still the core of the job and it is no longer all of it. I design the
images, the diagrams and the workflow visuals that go inside the posts. I build
and publish the pages themselves, working in Framer and in WordPress, and I look
after the CMS fields, the internal linking, and the migrations and redirects
when a site moves.

The same curiosity runs into the tools I write about. I have designed an n8n
workflow and I work in Clay, because writing well about a tool means knowing
what it feels like to build something in it.

Working with AI is what makes that range possible for one person. I can take a
page that already exists and shape it into whatever we agreed the site should
be, which is normally the kind of work that waits on a developer. Most of what I
do now started as something I had never done before and decided to try anyway.

I do this for [Nebor](https://nebor.ai), an agency in Amsterdam, while working
from Kigali in Rwanda. French and English are both native languages for me.
